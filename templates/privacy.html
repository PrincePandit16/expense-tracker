Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, and the expense data you log (amounts, categories, and descriptions). We may also collect basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to ensure the security and stability of our service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Spendly experience. This includes calculating your spending totals, generating category breakdowns, and managing your account. We do not use your personal financial data for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

We employ industry-standard security measures to store and protect your data. All account information is encrypted. You may request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may use trusted third-party hosting services to store our database, but these providers are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.example.com.

Your privacy is our priority.
